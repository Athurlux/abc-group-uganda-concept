# Pitch notes: ABC Group Uganda website concept

Prepared by Arthur Kitandwe to accompany https://abc-group-uganda-redesign.pages.dev. The concept is independent: ABC did not commission or approve it.

## Practical problems it addresses

These are based on reading abcgroupuganda.com on 19 October 2026.

1. **No way to collect choices.** A customer planning a bathroom has to remember items across pages, then describe them again in a WhatsApp message. The concept adds a project shortlist that keeps selections across visits and feeds them into a single enquiry.
2. **Enquiries arrive without context.** “Get a quote” opens WhatsApp with no information. The demo quote journey gathers the selections, project type, stage, location, timeframe and preferred showroom in a few short steps, so the first reply can be useful.
3. **Hard to tell what an image shows.** Some published labels don’t match their pictures: a “chrome faucet” that looks gold, an “oval bathtub” that is rectangular, a sink pack called single-bowl that has two bowls. The concept names items by what is visible, keeps the original label on record, and marks unknown details as “to be confirmed” rather than guessing.
4. **Five locations, little guidance.** The concept separates the four showrooms from the warehouse, gives each a page with its published phone number and a directions link, and lets a visitor choose a preferred showroom that carries into every form.
5. **Credibility details.** The current site gives two founding years (1998 and 2015), and its showroom photos carry old sale signage. The concept leaves out anything that can’t be confirmed and labels the photos as historical.

## Visual improvements

- A warm, architectural palette (limestone, charcoal, restrained bronze) that keeps **ABC’s own red logo** recognisable rather than replacing it.
- Editorial type (DM Sans, self-hosted), generous space, square-edged components and large photographic crops, with fewer boxes and borders.
- Product images shown whole on white, application photos cropped as scenes, and generated rooms clearly labelled as inspiration.
- Purposeful motion: a staged headline, an architectural image reveal, and one material-to-room moment where a limestone swatch grows into a room. All of it is disabled for reduced-motion users and simplified on phones.
- Mobile treated as first-class: copy above the hero image, two-column product grids, compact filters and a step indicator that collapses.

## How the shortlist-to-quote journey helps

- Customers save as they browse instead of keeping notes.
- Quantities are honest. Discrete products are counted in pieces; tiles, panels and adhesives stay “quantity to confirm”, because area and pack size need measuring. No totals or prices are invented.
- One enquiry carries the whole project, so ABC’s team can reply with relevant options and the right showroom.
- The same pattern supports professionals: a project brief can cover every collection a development needs.

## What an official launch would require

- **Approved content:** product names, descriptions, the range per showroom, opening hours, a confirmed founding year and project references with ABC’s actual role.
- **Verified catalogue data:** brands, models, finishes, dimensions, pack sizes, tile coverage, availability and any technical ratings, ideally from a maintained product list or an inventory export.
- **Image permissions:** confirmation that ABC holds the rights to product, brand and packaging images, and replacements for packaging images whose printed text looks garbled. New photography of products and showrooms would help most.
- **Real form delivery:** a server endpoint or CRM integration with spam protection, delivery monitoring and a reply process. The demo deliberately sends nothing.
- **Data handling:** a privacy notice, a retention policy for enquiries, consent where needed, and secure storage of personal details.
- **Technical launch work:** analytics chosen by ABC, search indexing (the concept is set to noindex), domain and hosting decisions, and accessibility testing with assistive technology on real devices.

## What this concept does not claim

It promises no conversion uplift, traffic growth or revenue effect, and it includes no testimonials, client endorsements, certifications, delivery guarantees or prices. Its value is a clearer, more trustworthy path from browsing to a well-formed enquiry, which ABC can measure after launch.
