# Content notes

## Scope and demo limits

An independent redesign proposal. Every form (quote, visit, project brief, contact) is a frontend demonstration. Nothing is transmitted, booked, quoted or stored on a server. The shortlist persists only catalogue IDs, piece counts and a preferred showroom in the visitor’s browser. Pages carry `noindex`. That reduces search confusion but does not make the deployment private.

## Unverified product fields

Brand, model, finish and colour, dimensions, technical specifications, price and availability are **not established for any item**. They are shown as “To be confirmed”, with a “Request product details” action, and are never filled in by inference. There are no slip ratings, wet-area claims, certifications, warranties, pack coverage, tile areas or delivery times anywhere on the site.

Brands named on ABC’s category pages are listed per collection as “brands named on ABC’s page”, with availability to be confirmed. They are not presented as partnerships, and there is no Brands page, because the primary sources name brands in captions and filenames without describing any relationship.

## Claims left out

- **Founding year:** the About page says both “Founded in 1998” and “Since 2015”, and the homepage says “Established in 2015”, so no year is stated.
- **Superlatives:** “Uganda’s largest…”, “the best sanitaryware”, “leading manufacturer” and similar are not repeated.
- **Counters:** the homepage impact counters weren’t confirmed (they may load by script), so no figures are used.
- **Projects:** the homepage names projects (for example Marriott Hotel Nsambya and Makerere University Ivory Tower) without stating ABC’s role. The About page lists a few names only, says the role is unconfirmed, and attaches no imagery or case study.
- **Service claims:** free consultations, budget planning, “24/7 premium support” and installation services are not repeated as promises.

## Editorial

Inspiration copy (room titles, ideas, finish notes) is original concept writing about generated rooms. It describes design ideas, not ABC products. The proposed line “Materials that make the space” and the headline “Every remarkable space starts with the right materials” come from the brief, not from ABC.
